About

I write content that ranks — and I manage the operation that produces it at scale.

My work sits where content writing meets search strategy. That means keyword and LSI research to find the openings, competitor content analysis to understand what already ranks and why, and then the writing itself: blog posts, landing pages, service pages and transactional copy built around those findings.

I optimise for more than classic search. Alongside SEO, I write for AEO (answer engines) and GEO (generative engines), so content stands up in featured snippets and AI-generated answers as well as on the results page. I also use prompt engineering and AI-assisted production to move faster without giving up quality or rankability.

After two years writing, I was promoted to Project Manager. I now coordinate content projects end to end — workflows, delivery and the quality standards the output is held to.
